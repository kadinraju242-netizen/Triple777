Origin & documentation

Every purchase
starts with questions.

Explore what the desk states about sourcing and ask for the documentation relevant to your lot.

Ask about a lot’s origin
01

Own production

Triple 7 mines, sorts and supplies from its own sites across Southern Africa. Lots listed on the desk come from that production or from partnered production the desk has assessed.

02

Natural and untreated

Stones listed as natural and untreated are stated as such on the lot itself. Where the desk does not state a characteristic, the lot page leaves it blank rather than assuming it.

03

Documentation on quote

Origin and processing documentation is provided by the desk to registered buyers during the quote process, so that it travels with a named counterparty rather than sitting on a public page.

04

Ask before you commit

If a specific document or assurance matters to your purchase, raise it in your quote request. The desk will tell you directly what it can and cannot provide for that lot.
