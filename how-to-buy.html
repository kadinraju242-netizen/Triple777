Explore LotsTalk to the desk
01

Browse

Open the Live Board and review the lots currently listed on the Kimberley desk. Every lot carries its identifier, commodity, type and the descriptors the desk publishes for it.

02

Register

Trading is limited to buyers known to the desk. Contact us to begin registration; the desk will confirm what it needs from you before a quote can be issued.

03

Request

Open a lot and request a quote on it. A request commits you to nothing and names no price.

04

Review

The Trade Desk reviews your request, confirms availability, and prepares commercial terms for the lot concerned.

05

Quote

The desk issues a written quote. Until that point no price exists — nothing on this site quotes a figure, because a figure has not been set.

06

Accept

Accept the quote, decline it, or ask the desk for changes. Terms are agreed in writing before anything moves.

07

Settle & complete

Settlement is arranged directly with the desk against the agreed terms, and final trade documentation follows. Settlement methods are confirmed per trade — this site does not process payment.

Quote requests are not offers. Submitting a request registers your interest with the Kimberley desk. It does not reserve a lot, create an obligation on either side, or set a price.
