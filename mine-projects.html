Start a project
with the division.

Triple 7 Holdings Mining Division partners with asset owners, technical operators, and capital providers to develop high-potential diamond and precious mineral projects across Southern Africa. We evaluate joint ventures, contract mining operations, and greenfield/brownfield development opportunities where our core strengths — operational execution, legal compliance, geological sorting, and access to our integrated Trade Desk — can maximise recovery and market value. Partners are expected to bring verified ground tenure, project capital, or specialised operational capacity.

01

Bring the opportunity

Ground, capital or operating capacity — tell us which you are bringing and where it sits. Submit details of your resource, existing mining permit or prospecting rights, funding commitment, or operational track record, along with the precise geographic region of the asset.

02

Initial review

Our geology, legal, and mining analyst teams perform an initial technical assessment and compliance check to review tenure validity, resource viability, and alignment with Triple 7’s ethical mining and safety standards.

What we will need

Before we can assess it.

The ground

Provide valid Mining Right or Prospecting Right details, GPS/cadastral coordinates, geological survey reports, historical sampling data, and current environmental compliance and permitting status.

Your position

Indicate whether you represent the property owner, licence holder, funding partner, or operating contractor, alongside your preferred joint-venture structure or partnership model.

Stage reached

Detail current project milestone status — from early-stage exploration and sampling, through bulk sampling, to fully permitted sites or operational mines seeking joint-venture expansion or Trade Desk access.

What is needed

Specify the key resources requested from Triple 7 Holdings, such as earthmoving equipment, technical geological oversight, processing infrastructure, working capital, or access to our direct trade and rough diamond export pathways.
