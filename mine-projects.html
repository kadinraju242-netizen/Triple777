What we will need

Before we can assess it.

Four things help us understand your project and how we can work together.

The ground

Mining or prospecting rights, site coordinates, geological reports and sampling results. Include current permits and environmental compliance details.

Your position

Tell us whether you are the owner, licence holder, investor or operator, and the partnership structure you have in mind.

Stage reached

Outline your progress: exploration, sampling, permitting or active mining. Share the milestones already completed.

What is needed

Set out the support you need: equipment, technical expertise, processing, working capital or access to diamond markets.
