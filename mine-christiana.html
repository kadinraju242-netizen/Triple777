The Site

Mining At Christiana Mine

Christiana is our footprint in the North West province, worked from ground near the Vaal. It extends the group beyond the Northern Cape and gives us a second production base with its own crew and its own local supplier network.

As with every Triple 7 Holdings site, production here is sold as natural, untreated, ethically-sourced rough — and we operate as a client of the State Diamond Trader, whose mandate is to hold up consumer confidence in South African diamonds.
