Purpose

A natural resource.
A shared responsibility.

Triple 7 Holdings is a diamond mining company located in South Africa. Our two mine projects are located in the Northern Cape — our goal is to produce high-quality large stones.

100% of the diamonds we sell are natural, untreated, and ethically sourced from various partnered mines. We operate transparently, in line with international best practice in sustainability, health, safety, environment, and community relations. Triple 7 Holdings is a client of the State Diamond Trader, which exists to maintain and enhance consumer confidence in diamonds.

Progress beyond
the mine.

Our mission is to provide infrastructure for education, healthcare, and housing for those who need it most. We achieve this by creating jobs in our communities through bursary schemes, and by working with small business owners to help grow their businesses.

We are introducing environmental schemes that lower our carbon footprint and contribute to the immediate environment, while empowering small business owners to engage with our organisation.
