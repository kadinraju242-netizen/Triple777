Who we are

South African roots.
A considered approach.

Triple 7 Holdings Mining Division is a South African diamond mining company built in Kimberley, with over a decade of industry experience.

Our work spans responsible resource extraction, high-quality rough diamonds and the advancement of cut and polished diamonds for the global market. Ethical practices, sustainable development and community empowerment guide our approach.

The diamonds we sell are natural, untreated and ethically sourced from our own and partnered operations.

Investor questions

A few useful answers.

Which mines can I discuss?

Bucklands in the Douglas area and Longlands in the Northern Cape. Approved investors can explore both projects in the investor workspace.

How much can I invest?

Contact the team for current funding requirements, minimum participation amounts if applicable, and the proposed terms. No standard investment amount is published here.

Can I invest directly on this website?

Register for access to the investor workspace. After approval, discuss participation with the team and review the proposed terms. Investments are not made or paid for on this website.

Can I request supporting documents?

Yes. Tell the team which project interests you and what information you need. They will explain what is available and any access requirements.
