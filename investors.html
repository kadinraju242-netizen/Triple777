Ask the Team

Questions answered

Before you enquire.

Which mines can I discuss?

Bucklands in the Douglas area and Longlands in the Northern Cape. Select either mine in your enquiry, or choose both if you would like to compare them.

How much can I invest?

Contact the team for current funding requirements, minimum participation amounts if applicable, and the proposed terms. No standard investment amount is published here.

Can I invest directly on this website?

The website is your starting point for information and enquiries. Participation is arranged with the team after you have reviewed the details and agreed the terms.

Can I request supporting documents?

Yes. Tell the team which project interests you and what information you need. They will explain what is available and any access requirements.

I am already an investor. Who can help?

Contact info@triple7holdings.co.za or call +27 21 300 1460 for investor support.
