Overview

Site readiness is the story: equipment in place, operations leadership engaged, and a pathway into sorting and Trade Desk supply once production cadence is established. Stones recovered here follow the same natural, untreated, ethically sourced standard as the rest of our portfolio.

Longlands sits at the practical edge of Triple 7’s South African pipeline — closer to production than greenfield, with a clear need for disciplined capital to unlock output.
