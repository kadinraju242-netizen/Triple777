Who we are

Built in Kimberley

Triple 7 Holdings Mining Division is a pioneering diamond mining company operating in South Africa. With a strong commitment to ethical practices, sustainable development, and community empowerment, we are positioned at the forefront of the diamond industry.

Our success is underpinned by a team of dedicated professionals led by our CEO, supported by finance and operations leadership that guides the company toward growth while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, and accountability.

With over a decade of industry experience, our focus spans responsible resource extraction — supplying high-quality rough diamonds, and advancing cut & polished diamonds, to the global market.

From the CEO

“As the CEO of Triple 7 Holdings Mining Division, I am proud to lead a team dedicated to innovative and ethical practices in the diamond industry. Our mission goes beyond business success — we strive to uplift communities, embody transparency, and shape a responsible future for mining. Through collaboration and responsible practices, we aim to make a positive impact on both the industry and the world.”

Daniel T. Ntsiyane

Vision & mission

Create value. Make a difference. Improve lives.

At Triple 7 Holdings Mining Division, our mission is clear: to create value, make a difference, and improve lives. Guided by our core values of Respect, Safety, Transparency, Collaboration, and Entrepreneurial Spirit, we empower our workforce to share innovative ideas that enhance our workplaces and the company as a whole. As we expand, we are dedicated to growing alongside the communities in which we operate. We invest in sustainable development initiatives by collaborating with local governments and communities, ensuring a positive impact on both social and economic levels.
